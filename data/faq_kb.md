# Kallang Auto Care: Customer Knowledge Base

Kallang Auto Care is an independent car servicing workshop in Kallang, Singapore. We service petrol, diesel, hybrid and fully electric passenger cars. This knowledge base is the official source of answers for our customer chat assistant. If a question is not covered here, the assistant should say so and offer to connect the customer with our service desk.

## Opening hours

Our workshop is open Monday to Friday from 8:30am to 6:00pm, and on Saturday from 8:30am to 1:00pm. We are closed on Sundays and on Singapore public holidays.

The last appointment slot on weekdays starts at 4:00pm, so that our technicians have time to finish the job and road-test your car before closing. On Saturdays the last appointment slot starts at 12:00pm.

Our service desk answers phone calls during opening hours only. Outside opening hours you can leave a message through this chat and a service advisor will reply on the next working day.

Car collection: if your car is ready after we close, it stays safely inside the workshop overnight and you can collect it from 8:30am the next working day. There is no storage charge for the first two days.

Customer parking: we have four customer lots directly in front of the workshop, marked with our logo. These lots are free for customers dropping off or collecting a car. Please do not park in the loading bay next to us, as it belongs to a neighbouring unit.

Waiting area: we have an air-conditioned waiting area with free drinks, charging points for phones and laptops, and free Wi-Fi. Short jobs such as tyre rotation can usually be completed while you wait.

## Servicing packages

We offer two servicing packages. Most manufacturers recommend servicing every 10,000 km or every six months, whichever comes first. Your owner's manual has the exact interval for your car.

The Basic servicing package (SGD 128) includes:

- Engine oil change with fully synthetic oil (up to 4.5 litres)
- New oil filter
- 20-point safety check covering lights, wipers, tyres, brakes, battery, belts and fluid levels
- Top-up of windscreen washer fluid and coolant
- Reset of the service reminder on your dashboard

The Full servicing package (SGD 268) includes everything in the Basic package, plus:

- New engine air filter
- New cabin (aircon) filter
- Spark plug check, and replacement quoted if needed
- Brake fluid top-up and brake performance test
- Battery health test with printed report
- Underbody check for leaks and worn parts
- Road test by a senior technician

Which package should I choose? If your last service was a Full servicing package, a Basic package is usually enough for the next interval. If you are unsure, or your car has done more than 60,000 km, we recommend the Full package. Our service advisor will check your service record when you drop off the car.

Servicing your car with us does not affect your manufacturer warranty, as long as the service follows the manufacturer's schedule and uses parts of matching quality. We stamp your service booklet and also send you a digital service record by email.

## EV and hybrid servicing

Electric and hybrid cars need fewer oil changes but still need regular checks. Our EV and hybrid health check (SGD 188) is carried out in a dedicated EV bay by technicians certified to work on high-voltage systems.

The health check includes:

- Traction battery health report, including estimated remaining capacity
- High-voltage cable and connector check
- Battery cooling system and coolant check
- Brake check: EVs use regenerative braking, so brake discs can rust from low use; we check and clean them
- Tyre check: EVs are heavier, so tyres tend to wear faster
- Software version check against the manufacturer's latest release

Hybrid cars also have a petrol engine, so they still need engine oil changes. For a hybrid, we recommend combining the EV and hybrid health check with a Basic servicing package. Book them as two separate services; we will schedule them in the same visit.

Charging: we have one 22 kW charger for customer cars being serviced. We will top up your battery while your car is with us, free of charge, but we cannot guarantee a full charge.

## Warranty

All workmanship carries a warranty of 6 months or 10,000 km, whichever comes first. If a problem comes back because of our work within that period, we will fix it at no cost to you.

Parts we supply carry the parts manufacturer's warranty, which is usually 12 months. Brake pads are covered against manufacturing defects but not against normal wear.

Detailing work: the protective coating from exterior detailing is guaranteed for 3 months. If the coating fails within that time under normal use, we will reapply it free of charge.

The warranty does not cover damage from accidents, misuse, modifications made by another workshop, or parts that you supplied yourself.

To use the warranty, contact our service desk with your booking reference. Please bring the car back to us rather than to another workshop, as repairs done elsewhere are not covered.

## What to bring

When you drop off your car, please bring:

- Your car key, plus the spare key if your car has a keyless or immobiliser system that needs it
- Your service booklet, if you have one, so we can stamp it
- The locking wheel nut key, if your car has locking wheel nuts (we cannot rotate or remove the wheels without it)
- Your booking reference, shown in your confirmation email

You do not need to bring your vehicle log card or any identity document. We only need the name and mobile number on the booking.

Please remove valuables from the car before drop-off. We are not responsible for personal items left in the car.

If someone else will drop off or collect the car for you, just tell them your booking reference. They do not need to show identification.

## Cancellation and no-show fees

You can cancel a booking free of charge up to 24 hours before the appointment time.

If you cancel less than 24 hours before the appointment, a late cancellation fee of SGD 20 applies. If you do not turn up and do not tell us, a no-show fee of SGD 30 applies. These fees are charged on your next visit.

We understand that emergencies happen. If you miss an appointment because of an emergency, tell our service desk and the fee may be waived.

If we need to cancel your appointment, for example because a technician is unwell, we will contact you as early as possible and offer the next available slot at no extra cost.

To cancel a booking, call our service desk or reply to your confirmation email with your booking reference. This chat assistant can take new bookings but cannot cancel existing ones yet.

## Courtesy car

For the Full servicing package, brake work and any job longer than 3 hours, you can request a courtesy car. We have two small hatchback courtesy cars, available on a first-come, first-served basis.

To use a courtesy car you must be at least 23 years old and have held a valid driving licence for at least two years. You will need to show your driving licence when you collect the courtesy car.

The courtesy car is free for the day of your service. Please return it with the same fuel level. Parking fees, ERP charges and traffic fines incurred while you use it are your responsibility.

Courtesy cars must be requested at least one working day in advance. Ask our service desk after you have made your booking.

## Rescheduling

You can move your appointment to another date or time free of charge up to 24 hours before the original appointment. Rescheduling less than 24 hours before is treated as a late cancellation and the SGD 20 fee applies, unless the new slot is on the same day.

To reschedule, call our service desk or reply to your confirmation email with your booking reference and your preferred new date and time. This chat assistant can take new bookings but cannot change existing ones yet.

If your car is already with us and the job will take longer than expected, for example because a part needs to be ordered, our service advisor will call you with the new completion time before continuing.

## Data and privacy notice

Kallang Auto Care collects only the personal data we need to manage your booking: your name, mobile number, email address and vehicle registration number.

We use this data to confirm your booking, send reminders, contact you about your car during the service, and keep a service record for your vehicle. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for marketing unless you separately agree to receive offers.

We do not collect your NRIC number, passport number or payment card details through this chat. Please do not share them here.

Your booking data is kept for 2 years after your last visit so we can keep your service history, and is then deleted.

You can ask to see, correct or withdraw consent for the use of your personal data by emailing our Data Protection Officer at dpo@example.com. Withdrawing consent may mean we cannot take further bookings for you.

This notice is provided in line with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).
